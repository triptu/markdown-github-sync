# Attention is the real budget

Money and time get budgets. Attention doesn't, and it's the one that runs out first.

- a meeting costs more attention than its length
- an open loop costs attention every time you remember it
- notifications are tiny withdrawals all day

If I budgeted attention like rent, what would I cut? Probably the second news app, and half of the [[Ideas to build]] list.
