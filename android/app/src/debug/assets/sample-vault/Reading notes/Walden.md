# Walden — Henry David Thoreau

**Finished:** late October 2025
**Rating:** 4/5, with long boring stretches about beans

## Why I picked it up

Everyone quotes it. Wanted to know if the quotes were the good parts or if there was more. Mostly the quotes are the good parts, but the in-between is where you get to know him.

## Big ideas

### Simplicity
"Our life is frittered away by detail... Simplify, simplify."

He's not saying own nothing. He's saying notice what each thing costs you in *life*, not money. That reframing stuck with me for weeks. A thing costs the hours you worked to buy it, plus the hours you spend looking after it.

### Deliberate living
"I went to the woods because I wished to live deliberately."

The word that matters is *deliberately*. Not "simply" or "alone". You could live deliberately in a city flat. I think that's the actual lesson.

### Economy
The first chapter is basically a budget spreadsheet. He lists what the cabin cost, down to the nails. Weirdly the most modern part of the book.

- house: about 28 dollars
- he grew beans to sell, made a small profit, then decided it wasn't worth it
- he's honest that his mother still did his laundry (well, the book doesn't say that, but people love to point it out)

## Chapters I liked

1. **Economy** — the budget stuff
2. **Where I Lived, and What I Lived For** — the famous bits
3. **Solitude** — about being alone without being lonely
4. **The Pond in Winter** — measuring the depth of the pond, refusing to believe it's bottomless
5. **Spring** — the thawing sand bank, pure joy

## Chapters I skimmed

- Higher Laws (too preachy)
- Baker Farm
- most of The Bean-Field, sorry

## Quotes

> "The mass of men lead lives of quiet desperation."

> "Rather than love, than money, than fame, give me truth."

> "Heaven is under our feet as well as over our heads."

> "I had three chairs in my house; one for solitude, two for friendship, three for society."

That last one is my favorite. Three chairs. That's the whole guest policy.

## Disagreements

- He's a bit smug about people who work for a living
- Solitude is easier when your friends live a short walk away
- The book is about two years but reads like one, which is a trick, and he admits it
