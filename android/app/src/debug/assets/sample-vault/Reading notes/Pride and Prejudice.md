# Pride and Prejudice — Jane Austen

Reread for comfort. It works every time.

- Elizabeth is funny in a way that still lands
- Mr. Bennet is the best character and also a pretty bad father
- the letter chapter is the turning point, and it's just someone admitting they were wrong, in writing

> "I could easily forgive his pride, if he had not mortified mine."

**Takeaway:** first impressions are a draft. Revise them.
