# Siddhartha — Hermann Hesse

Short, read it in two evenings.

The river is the whole book. He tries teachers, then pleasure, then business, and ends up learning from a ferryman who just listens to the water.

> "I can think, I can wait, I can fast."

That's his whole résumé when he goes looking for work, and I love it.

Felt related to [[What the lake taught me]]. Water as a teacher. Maybe I'm just looking for excuses to walk.
