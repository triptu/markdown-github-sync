# Meditations — Marcus Aurelius

Reading it a second time (first was five years ago). Gregory Hays translation.

## Lines that hit again

> "You have power over your mind — not outside events. Realize this, and you will find strength."

> "The impediment to action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way."

> "Waste no more time arguing what a good man should be. Be one."

## Notes

- it's a private journal, he never meant it to be read. you can feel that. he repeats himself, scolds himself
- book 2 opens with "when you wake up in the morning, tell yourself: the people i deal with today will be meddling, ungrateful..." — honestly a decent pre-standup ritual
- the bits about death are calmer than i remembered

## Compare

[[Walden]] is about building your life on purpose. Meditations is about keeping your head while the life happens to you.
