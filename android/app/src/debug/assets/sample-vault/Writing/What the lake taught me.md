# What the lake taught me

*draft 1*

I walk around the lake most evenings. It takes forty minutes if I don't stop, and I always stop.

Things I've noticed:

1. The ducks have a schedule. They're at the north end at 6 and the south end by 7.
2. The same old man feeds them. He nods at me now.
3. On days I'm angry, I walk faster, and the lake gets smaller.

The lake doesn't care about any of my problems, which is the most restful thing about it.
