# Every productivity system eventually collapses under its own weight

Third time this year I've rebuilt the task setup from scratch. The pattern is always the same: a clean start, two good weeks, then the system needs more care than the work it was meant to organise.

Maybe the fix is to keep it too small to collapse. One list, one note per week, nothing that needs a weekly review to stay alive.
