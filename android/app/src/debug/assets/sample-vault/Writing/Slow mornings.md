# Slow mornings

*short piece, maybe for the newsletter*

I used to treat mornings like a runway: get up, get going, get speed. Now I treat them like a porch.

Coffee outside. No phone until the cup is empty. One page of something, written or read.

It's not a productivity hack. Some days nothing comes of it. That's sort of the point.

Grew out of [[2025102607 Mornings belong to one thing]].
