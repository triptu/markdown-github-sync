# Why I stopped tracking everything

*blog draft, ~600 words so far*

For two years I tracked sleep, steps, water, words written, pages read, coffee, and mood on a 1–5 scale. I had charts. The charts were beautiful.

I didn't change anything because of them.

## What went wrong

Tracking felt like doing. Filling in the mood score felt like taking care of my mood. It wasn't.

## What I kept

- **pages read**, because it's fun to watch the number go up
- a weekly note, because writing is the only tracking that talks back

## What I dropped

Everything else. Nothing bad happened.

## Ending (rough)

The point of noticing your life is to live it differently. If the noticing becomes the life, stop.
